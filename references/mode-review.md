# Mode-specific review

Review the selected surface first, then the visual job and the family skeleton.

## Surface fit

Does the field temperature, diffusion, print behavior, and texture host match the internally selected rendering branch? Did an unselected retro, sepia, neon, or sterile UI treatment leak in?

## Canonical cover fit

Unless the user explicitly chose another mode, check:

- dominant upper title plane;
- compact dark-anchored central event;
- visible unequal contacting meso field;
- soft material layer against crisp printed structure;
- authored lower/lateral release.

## Mode fit

- **Hero:** one subject or event is unmistakably first; supports stay subordinate.
- **Material:** surface, transformation, wear, light, or construction carries the hierarchy.
- **Process:** stages and causal order are understandable.
- **Archive:** collection or comparison logic is intelligible.
- **Narrative:** place, time, action, and figure-ground reading lead.
- **Information:** semantic labels, grouping, sequence, or evidence are readable.
- **Quiet:** calm and shaped emptiness are explicitly the task.

## Diagnose before iterating

- Router failure: the viewer job is wrong.
- Recipe failure: the job is right but pressure distribution is wrong.
- Execution failure: route is right but title, subject, contact, material, palette, or the chosen generator’s rendering is weak.

Change only the top one or two failed blocks. Do not repair a routing failure with more adjectives.
