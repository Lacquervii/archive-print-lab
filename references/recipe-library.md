# Recipe library — pressure profiles

Select the visual job before writing the image prompt. The default is the canonical concentrated editorial cover.

```text
mode: concentrated_cover
recipe: concentrated_editorial_cover
chassis: structural
```

Use an alternate recipe only when the user explicitly asks for it:

- `hero` — one singular subject or event must lead immediately;
- `material` — surface, wear, transformation, light, or physical credibility is the message;
- `process` — making, assembly, repair, or stages must be followed;
- `archive` — collecting, indexing, comparing, or documenting is the task;
- `narrative` — place, time, action, or atmosphere is primary;
- `information` — evidence, sequence, labels, axes, or explanation must be understood;
- `quiet` — calm and broad emptiness are explicitly requested.

Do not infer an alternate route from a subject noun alone. A book, flower, object, or computer remains a canonical cover unless the user changes the viewer job.

## Concentrated editorial cover

The standard pressure profile is:

```text
upper title plane → compact unequal subject family → visible contacting field → authored release
```

Keep the central subject complete, the footprint bounded, and the field unequal. Use short image-native title text and a small number of meaningful marks.

## Subject-to-recipe check

Before prompt assembly, ask:

1. What must the viewer notice first?
2. Is the subject one event, a family, a process, a material state, a scene, or an information system?
3. Should typography be structural, explanatory, indexical, optional, or absent?
4. Where does density belong?
5. What kind of release or pause does the carrier need?

If no explicit override is present, retain the canonical cover. Do not let a recent successful image decide the recipe.
