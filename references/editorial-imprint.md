# Editorial imprint — Archive Print Lab series signature

## Purpose

The canonical Archive Print Lab family carries a small publication-like imprint in generated covers. This is a **micro editorial credit / colophon**, not a watermark, logo, product mark, or second title. It gives the series a quiet authored signature without competing with the subject.

## Default imprint

```text
text: archive-print-lab
case: lowercase
punctuation: hyphenated exactly as shown
role: small editorial imprint / publication credit
```

When rendering a canonical cover, include a tiny visible text treatment reading **“archive-print-lab”**. Image-native generators may render the glyphs approximately; report that limitation honestly and keep the imprint subordinate.

## Placement and behavior

- Place it in the connected lower-right release or a quiet side information lane, aligned with an existing rule, fading contour, study label, or registration axis.
- Keep it small, low-contrast, widely tracked, and materially consistent with the selected finish.
- It may sit beside or below a short study/edition label, but it must remain subordinate to the title, central subject, and meso field.
- Let it feel like a typeset imprint from the same printed object: quiet, slightly ornamental, useful for authorship and series continuity.
- Do not turn it into a large logo, badge, watermark, URL, signature scribble, headline, or isolated floating text.
- Do not place it over the focal subject or use it to fill an accidental blank area.

## Surface translation

- `warm_analog_print`: warm charcoal/olive or muted umber ink, slight registration softness.
- `contemporary_frosted`: graphite-olive or mineral gray, matte and restrained.
- `crisp_modern_graphic`: precise dark neutral, clean rule alignment.
- `material_archive`: ink matched to the material record while retaining low contrast.
- `custom`: follow the user-selected surface, but preserve the small editorial role.

## Prompt wording

Use observable language such as:

> In the lower-right release, add a tiny, widely tracked lowercase editorial imprint reading “archive-print-lab”, aligned to the short rule and clearly secondary to the main title.

The imprint is a default series signature for generated canonical covers. An explicit user request to omit it overrides this rule.
