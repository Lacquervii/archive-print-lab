# Structural scale ladder — canonical pressure, backend-neutral

## Scope

This card governs the ordinary `concentrated_cover` / `concentrated_editorial_cover` route. It is a reusable **pressure contract**, not a literal inventory of objects and not a requirement for hero, material, process, narrative, information or quiet overrides.

A convincing Archive Print Lab cover must survive image-native rendering as a hierarchy of unequal visual roles:

```text
macro title plane
→ broad compact central event
→ unequal primary / support / return field
→ localized dark anchor
→ sparse subject-index evidence
→ authored lower-right release + series imprint
```

The family loses its identity when theme customization deletes the middle pressure and leaves only a title, one polite subject, and a faint line.

## 1. Macro title plane

The title is a real upper image event, not a neat caption.

- Give it roughly one-third to one-half of the page's visual mass, targeting about two-fifths.
- Leave a small clean breathing gap of approximately 3–5% of the canvas height above it.
- Keep the word recoverable and broadly contained with narrow definite side margins; contained does not mean timid or small.
- Let it span most of the upper field and, when useful, enter the upper middle.
- Let the complete central event visibly interrupt its lower edge.
- If it reads like a header, increase its visual authority before adding more subject detail.

The exact glyphs remain generator-dependent. Short, meaningful display words are preferred; do not promise pixel-perfect typography.

## 2. Broad compact central event

The central subject is a rich designed event held inside a controlled outer envelope.

- one complete dominant gestalt;
- unequal related whole forms, echoes, states, shadows or material returns;
- front / middle / rear depth;
- meaningful overlap or contained crop;
- at least one localized dark anchor or pressure pocket;
- material evidence that rewards normal and close viewing;
- a generally lateral middle belt for wide covers, with axial stems, trunks, spines or page axes countered by lateral forms or cross-structure.

Richness belongs inside the envelope. Do not expand the silhouette until it consumes the title, margins or release; do not shrink it into an isolated icon merely to preserve whitespace.

## 3. Meso field pressure floor

Every canonical structural cover must compile one readable unequal field at normal viewing distance. It has three related roles:

1. **primary carrier** — the main plane, disc, contour, shadow, path or printed field;
2. **support carrier** — an unequal block, plane, path, echo or counter-pressure;
3. **return carrier** — a member that disappears behind/under the subject and reappears toward the release.

These roles may be theme-native, carrier-native, profile-native, or mixed. Re-author their direction, scale, opacity, palette, material host and contact for the current theme. They are not a demand for copied rectangles, purple frames, stars, fake data or generic HUD furniture.

The field must:

- be visible without zooming in;
- have unequal scale, weight, opacity or depth;
- pass behind, be cut by, align with, or reappear around the complete subject;
- share at least three readable contacts with title, subject, dark anchor or release;
- remain subordinate to the central gestalt while still carrying family-level page pressure;
- continue, fade or resolve into the authored release.

Do not reduce a canonical field to one faint line, one isolated circle, equal repeated furniture, or micro labels pretending to be structure.

## 4. Localized dark pressure

Use contrast selectively rather than darkening the entire page. A seed disk, cavity, fold, shadow junction, deep overlap, printed block or material recess can anchor the middle. The dark anchor should be spatially connected to the subject or field and should help the title-to-center reading path.

## 5. Micro layer and series imprint

Micro marks close the reading loop; they do not create the composition by themselves.

- Use only a few short subject-relevant labels, a scale/axis gesture, registration marks or edition cues when they have a page job.
- For canonical organic covers, two to four short index words and one or two restrained registration gestures are a useful starting point, not a rigid quota.
- Align marks to actual forms, carrier edges or the release axis. Never invent fake factual metadata to fill space.
- In the lower-right release, attempt the tiny lowercase, widely tracked imprint **`archive-print-lab`**. It is a publication-like colophon and decorative editorial detail, not a watermark, logo, badge, URL or second title.

Image-native models may approximate fine text. Keep the imprint visually small and secondary; do not claim exact spelling from generated pixels.

## 6. Contact matrix

Before rendering, choose at least three distinct relations from the following family:

- the subject interrupts the lower edge of the title;
- a primary plane passes behind the subject;
- a support carrier is cut by a leaf, petal, object, shadow or material edge;
- a carrier disappears beneath the central event and reappears at the side or lower field;
- a dark anchor sits behind or inside a lighter material mass;
- a label or scale aligns to a real contour or carrier edge;
- the return field fades into the release beside the `archive-print-lab` imprint.

Do not make every layer merely transparent and co-planar. Contact needs a readable front/back decision, interruption, occlusion or shared alignment.

## 7. Backend translation

This ladder is backend-neutral. When the user names Image2/gpt-image-2, additionally load `image2-editorial-adapter.md`; it translates the pressure floor into natural spatial prose and preserves the imprint attempt. When the user names another generator, adapt syntax and parameters without deleting the roles. When no generator is named, deliver the compiled prompt and keep the same visual contract.

## Review scale

- **Thumbnail:** title authority, central mass, unequal field pressure and release are legible.
- **Normal distance:** primary/support/return carriers and front/back contacts appear as one coherent printed field.
- **Close range:** material proof, grain, registration and small imprint feel hosted by the same designed print.

If a result is attractive but only contains a large title, a generic subject and decorative micro lines, classify it as a structural regression and repair the composition/adapter—not by adding adjectives.
