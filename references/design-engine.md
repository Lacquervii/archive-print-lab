# Design engine

## Identity

Archive Print Lab is a system of relationships, not a bag of recurring objects, colors, or decorations.

```text
hierarchy + scale contrast + contact + material cause + fresh palette + release
```

For the default cover, the stable reading event is:

```text
macro title → compact central subject → contacting meso field → quiet release
```

## Transferable decisions

Keep these relationships across themes:

- one clear first attention event;
- meaningful scale contrast instead of equal repetition;
- a bounded but internally rich central event;
- soft material volume against crisp printed structure;
- visible overlap, interruption, alignment, or reappearance between layers;
- localized dark pressure rather than global darkness;
- a composed quiet zone;
- texture attached to a real host: paper, ink, glass, bark, cloth, metal, light, or subject surface.

## Re-author every time

Do not carry these by inertia:

- the subject and its supporting forms;
- field temperature and color roles;
- title word and ink;
- plane direction and structural vocabulary;
- material proof;
- density distribution and release trace;
- refusal list.

## Anti-patterns

Reject a result that becomes:

- a source image with its noun swapped;
- an isolated object on a polite pale field;
- a generic bouquet, lineup, still life, or scene;
- a default purple frame system or decorative technical furniture;
- global blur, global black, global sepia, or texture with no host;
- an attractive generated image presented with claims its pixels do not support.
