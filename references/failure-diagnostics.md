# Failure diagnostics

| Symptom | Likely cause | Targeted repair |
|---|---|---|
| Generic still life | subject was written before carrier mechanics | rebuild macro, central family, meso contact, and release first |
| Bouquet or lineup | equal repetition and no dominant family | create one dominant whole form, unequal supports, overlap, and crop |
| Title is a caption | typography was added after the subject | enlarge the short title and let the subject interrupt its lower edge |
| Marks feel pasted on | no theme, carrier, or chassis page job | remove unresolved marks or make them pass behind, align with, or reappear around the subject |
| Center feels polite | generic furniture was removed without energy substitution | add theme-native depth, echoes, material density, and localized dark anchors |
| Center is too full | outer footprint expanded to solve weak pressure | reduce the envelope; retain internal richness |
| Meso field disappears | only micro marks were added | introduce one or two unequal medium-scale planes, shadows, contours, or material transitions |
| Whole image is too dark | global contrast was used as pressure | keep the field pale and localize dark anchors |
| Whole image is too pale | recesses and material boundaries were erased | restore localized shadows, cavities, seams, or selected color events |
| Wrong atmosphere | surface branch was not resolved or leaked from another theme | reselect the branch and rewrite only the surface clause |
| Wrong ratio claim | requested size was reported as actual size | inspect the returned file and report its true dimensions |
| Text looks wrong | too much or too long text was requested from the generator | shorten displayed words and accept image-native approximation |
| Subject looks copied from an old case | historical output was loaded for a fresh request | restart from the current brief and Skill only |
| Many elements but no design | elements were counted instead of related | remove uncaused elements and strengthen hierarchy/contact |

Change only the top one or two failing dimensions per iteration.
